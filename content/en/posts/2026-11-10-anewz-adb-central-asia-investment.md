On 10 November 2026, Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla joined AnewZ live as an independent expert for a segment on the Asian Development Bank's new study on investment potential in Central Asia and the Caucasus. The discussion covered the study's four sectors, investment and regional connectivity, and cooperation among the countries concerned.

{{< preview url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kpDjgftcmk&t=1810s" title="Live commentary, segment 30:10–41:11" site="AnewZ" image="images/media/anewz-2026-11-adb-investment-outlook.jpg" >}}

He noted that the study's $2.9–3.8 trillion figure is an energy investment need across the CAREC region by 2030, and that predictable, bankable projects can close that gap. He also pointed to a shared register of cross-border projects as a practical first step.
