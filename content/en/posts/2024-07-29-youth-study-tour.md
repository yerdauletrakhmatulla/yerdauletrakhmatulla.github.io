In 2024, twenty young people from Kazakhstan and Germany spent a week travelling through Kazakhstan to learn about the country's nuclear past and its consequences today. Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla co-organised the tour with the Friedrich Ebert Foundation in Kazakhstan, ICAN Germany and the Steppe Organization for Peace (STOP).

In Astana, the group met officials at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and visited Nazarbayev University. In Almaty, participants joined discussions with activists and experts and a documentary screening. In Semey, they met communities affected by Soviet nuclear testing, the Abai Region administration and local research institutions, including Semey Medical University. During the week the group also met a judge of the Constitutional Court and a member of Parliament.

For him, the tour also confirmed how much intergenerational dialogue matters, and how much regional activists and affected communities need support to build their own capacity.
