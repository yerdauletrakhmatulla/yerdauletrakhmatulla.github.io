On 15 November 2026, Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla attended the third and final day of the 2026 Seoul International People's Tribunal on the 1945 US Atomic Bombings. The Tribunal ran on 13–15 November at the Graduate School of Theology, Hanshin University, in Seoul. It was organized by SPARK (Solidarity for Peace and Reunification of Korea) with an international organizing committee. Korean atomic bomb victims appeared as plaintiffs before a panel of international judges. It is a civil society initiative.

The proceedings were a historic occasion. Thanks go to SPARK and the organizing team. Recognition is due to the judges, the plaintiffs and everyone who contributed to the proceedings.
