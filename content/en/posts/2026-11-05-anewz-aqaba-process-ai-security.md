On 5 November 2026, Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla joined AnewZ live as a guest analyst to comment on the Aqaba Process meeting in Astana on 6 November, co-chaired by President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev and King Abdullah II of Jordan.

{{< preview url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rj3izMdZtEw&t=2352s" title="Live commentary, segment 39:12–47:26" site="AnewZ" image="images/media/anewz-2026-11-aqaba-ai-security.jpg" >}}

He noted that AI mainly changes the speed, scale and cost of existing terrorist tactics, such as multilingual propaganda and tailored recruitment messaging. The UN's 2025 assessment found no confirmed AI-enabled attack so far, while the experts it surveyed consider one likely.

For Central Asia, he pointed to multilingual content, a young online population and labour migrants abroad as the main exposure. Digital literacy and counter-narratives in local languages matter as much as takedowns. Practical steps he named were cooperation between national CERTs, joint exercises, and shared standards for deepfake labelling and AI incident reporting, building on the UN Joint Plan of Action for Central Asia.

On President Tokayev's proposed regional declaration on responsible AI, he said it would gain from a security chapter, an incident-sharing commitment and a review mechanism. Security measures should rest on legality, necessity, proportionality and oversight, because public trust is itself a security asset.
