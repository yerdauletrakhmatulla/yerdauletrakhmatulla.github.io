Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla joined AnewZ live on 29 October 2026 as a guest analyst. He commented on remarks by CSTO Secretary-General Taalatbek Masadykov, who said that Central Asian governments, both members and non-members of the organisation, have turned down Western requests to host military bases in the region.

{{< preview url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_0GiRdGFMOA&t=2070s" title="Live commentary, segment 34:30–42:40" site="AnewZ" image="images/media/anewz-2026-10-western-bases.jpg" >}}

**Key points from the discussion**

- **A long-standing pattern.** US forces left the region in 2014, and no Central Asian government has invited a Western military presence back since. He noted that the account of Western requests comes from the CSTO and has not been publicly confirmed by Western governments.

- **Different routes, one conclusion.** Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan fall under the CSTO rule requiring all members to approve any non-member base. Uzbekistan bans foreign bases in its foreign policy concept and military doctrine. Turkmenistan's permanent neutrality rules them out. He described the outcome as a convergence among states with different alliances.

- **Specific threats, specific tools.** On Afghanistan, he separated three risks: ISKP networks, which the UN monitoring team says remain focused on Central Asia; instability along the Tajik-Afghan border; and a drug trade shifting from opium to methamphetamine. These call for border capacity and intelligence sharing. The CSTO's programme to strengthen the Tajik-Afghan border, which runs until 2029, will be judged on delivery.

- **Engagement with firm borders.** He pointed to growing trade and transport links with Afghanistan, including Kazakhstan's planned Torghundi–Herat railway, as reasons for pragmatic engagement backed by strong border management.

His overall reading was that security in Central Asia is increasingly decided in Central Asia, through border settlements, regional security formats, and partnerships each government chooses on its own terms.

This appearance follows his earlier AnewZ commentary on the [20th anniversary of the Central Asian Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zone](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/yerdaulet_primetime-nuclearweaponfreezone-ugcPost-7504132932496891904-kYts/), [President Tokayev's visit to Germany](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zv8RjNDFXvA&t=2610s) and [the CIS summit in Awaza](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EvsUCXZk_tE&t=2003s).
