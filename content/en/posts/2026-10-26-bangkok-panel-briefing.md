On 20 October 2026, Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla briefed the UN Independent Scientific Panel on the Effects of Nuclear War on the impacts of nuclear explosions in Kazakhstan, at the Panel's Asia-Pacific Regional Consultations in Bangkok.

The General Assembly set up the Panel in December 2024. It is due to report to the General Assembly in 2027, in the first UN study of its kind since 1988. Its mandate includes hearing from affected communities and civil society, whose evidence complements models and datasets.

Building on presentations by colleagues, Yerdaulet widened the lens to nuclear explosions elsewhere in Kazakhstan and across the region.

Yerdaulet is grateful to Chris King and Erika Kawahara of the Panel's secretariat at the UN Office for Disarmament Affairs for organising the consultations, and to the Panel experts in Bangkok, Togzhan Kassenova, Karina Meredith, Sébastien Philippe, Ausrele Kesminiene-Suonio and Md Ahsan Habib, for their questions.
