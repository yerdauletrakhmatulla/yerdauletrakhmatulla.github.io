On 13 November 2026, Yerdaulet Rakhmatulla spoke in Session 5, "Shaping the Future: Next-generation perspectives on AI, disarmament and arms control", at the 25th ROK-UN Joint Conference on Disarmament and Non-Proliferation Issues. He took part as a member of the Youth Delegation.

The Conference took place on 12–13 November 2026 at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Korea in Seoul. It was convened by the Ministry and the UN Office for Disarmament Affairs (UNODA), and prepared by UNODA's Regional Centre for Peace and Disarmament in Asia and the Pacific (UNRCPD). The 2026 edition marked 25 years of partnership between the Republic of Korea and the United Nations. Its theme was the cross-cutting implications of artificial intelligence for disarmament, non-proliferation and arms control.

As described in the UN press release of 12 November, the meeting brought together representatives of Governments, international organizations and experts from academia, civil society and industry. Opening remarks were scheduled from Yeondoo Jeong, Vice-Minister for Strategy and Intelligence of the Republic of Korea, and Izumi Nakamitsu, UN Under-Secretary-General and High Representative for Disarmament Affairs. Sessions addressed nuclear risk, chemical and biological weapons, cybersecurity, and the use of AI in conventional military operations, including targeting and autonomous functions. Session 5 was the dedicated session on next-generation perspectives, with young researchers, practitioners and youth representatives. The release notes the Conference's recognition that young people need a meaningful role in shaping disarmament policy.

The Conference was closed-door, so this note does not report on the discussions. The programme offered the opportunity to meet and learn from experts in these fields. Thanks go to the Ministry, UNODA and UNRCPD for the opportunity to take part, and to the experts and fellow Youth Delegation members for the exchanges during the programme.
